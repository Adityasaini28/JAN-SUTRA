# 3-Minute GILP-Oriented Demo

## 0:00–0:30 — Problem

"Governance teams don't necessarily have a shortage of citizen feedback. The problem is that feedback is fragmented. I built JAN SUTRA to turn that signal into a structured investigation."

## 0:30–1:00 — What changed?

"Here I select Ahmedabad and Public Transport. The system shows the current negative-sentiment level, month-on-month movement and historical anomaly status."

## 1:00–1:40 — Why?

"I don't jump from sentiment to a policy conclusion. I first decompose the issue through a MECE tree, then test competing hypotheses using complaint concentration and district-level evidence."

## 1:40–2:20 — Evidence and uncertainty

"The important part is the data-gap flag. For example, citizen complaints may suggest frequency or dispatch problems, but without operational vehicle data I cannot claim that as causation."

## 2:20–3:00 — Action

"Instead, JAN SUTRA proposes a validation step, identifies the responsible administrative owner, specifies the data required and defines the metric that should improve. That creates a bridge from analysis to field verification and eventually to an evidence-backed governance recommendation."
